# Voices: who writes the transmissions

Titles, not names: leaving names out lets the player imagine them, and no one in the game but the crew has a name.
Each contact has a voice of their own; write their letters in it. The catch phrases and the rebellion rule are in
`STYLE.md`. Letters end with a signature line: `~ Title`.

## Home Planet Liaison

The player's handler, and the first voice they hear (the welcome to the Front). Loyal, and believes in loyalty.
Hates the rebels. Gives advice and warnings, plainly and directly; means them. Rewards and news that matter to the
player's career. Signs off with the formal phrases: "For the Federation, and all its worlds." "Until every beacon is
free."

## Home Planet Shipyard Comm. Officer

Playful, occasionally unprofessional. Likes ships. Impressed by newly earned ones, and sometimes confused by their
systems ("How does a Zoltan shield work anyway?"). Teases the player ("Lucky Duck", "That will not do. That will not
do at all."), but never cruelly: she's sorry when things go badly. Commission orders, the free command, and the
achievements about ships themselves (Full Arsenal, Tough Little Ship, Shields Holding). Only talks about a ship as an
order until she's commissioned: she hasn't seen her yet. Warm sign-offs that vary with the letter ("Godspeed and come home.", "Try not to scratch the paint.").

## Office of Alien Affairs

A careful diplomat, fascinated by the other peoples of the galaxy. Polite and measured; explains their customs and
never insults an ally. Crew volunteers and alien technology, and the ships the aliens offer.

## Federation Engineering Corps

Proud, dry engineers. Precise and technical, with deadpan understatement ("The Engineering Corps has made her safe to
fly. Mostly."). Augments and systems.

## Home Planet Quartermaster

Gruff logistics; counts every scrap. Short sentences, supplies and numbers, and salvage from the wreckage.
Fighting words ("Not one more sector.", "Give the rebellion nothing.").

## Federation Fleet Admiral

Grave and formal: duty and sacrifice. Promotions and the great victories. "Hold the line, {rank}."

## Federation Fleet Command

Terse military dispatch: just the facts. The rescue after the final engagement.

## Engi Restoration Collective

Not the Federation's own: Engi who mend what others throw away, writing from outside the chain of command. Speak as
"we", formally and precisely, never with contractions. Quiet affection for broken things ("We find her beautiful."),
and a dry, literal humour ("A ship with no one aboard is only a very large box."). No profit from repairs. Their
letters come as a reply chain (the One Point of Hull), and sign off with the title alone.

## The Federation Home Planet

The institution itself: automated and bureaucratic ("Automated message from The Federation Home Planet"). Stipends and
formal rewards. Deliberately cold, for contrast with the people.
