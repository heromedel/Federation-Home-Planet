Federation Home Planet
for FTL: Faster Than Light 1.6

The Home Planet Station keeps your FTL fleet between runs: a Space Dock of ships to board and fly, a Cargo Bay to
refit them, a Cargo Hold for what they bring home, new ships commissioned by The Federation Home Planet, and crews
sent out on expeditions.


WHAT YOU NEED

- FTL: Faster Than Light 1.6 (Steam, GOG or Humble).
- Windows.
- Java 8 or newer. If it isn't on your computer, the station offers to get it for you.


STARTING THE STATION

1. Unzip this folder anywhere you like.
2. Double-click "Federation Home Planet Interface.bat".

If no Java is found, the window offers two choices:
  1: Download Java just for the station, into a "java" folder beside it. Nothing is installed, and deleting
     that folder removes it.
  2: Download and install Java for the whole computer.


THE FIRST START

The Home Planet Station asks a few things once:

1. FTL's saves folder. It usually finds it by itself; if not, it asks you to choose it.
2. FTL's own folder (where FTL is installed). Confirm the one it found, or choose it.
3. Steam version only: whether to launch FTL through Steam. Then a reminder to turn off Steam Cloud for FTL
   (in your Steam library: right-click FTL, Properties, General). With it on, Steam can bring back old copies
   of your ships.
4. Slipstream Mod Manager. Refitted, remodeled and designed ships are sent to FTL via Slipstream. Choose
   Browse... if you have it already, Download it for me, or Not now: everything else works without it, and
   you can set it up later in Settings.
5. Sandbox Mode or Immersive Mode. Sandbox Mode is FTL as you know it, with rules you choose. Immersive Mode
   is a Federation career, at a difficulty chosen when it begins. Then many of the rules are locked in and can't
   be changed. (Unlike Sandbox)

The Mode you are in and folder paths and other options can be changed later in Settings.


WHERE YOUR FLEET IS KEPT

Your fleets and the station's settings are kept in FTL's saves folder (in the folders whose names start with
FederationHomePlanet), not beside the program. A new version can be unzipped anywhere and finds everything
without asking again. To back up your fleets, back up FTL's saves folder.

To remove the station, delete this folder. Your fleets stay in FTL's saves folder until you delete those
FederationHomePlanet folders too.
If you were still boarded on a ship it will be left as your continue for FTL as the continue.sav

If you began an Immersive career with its own FTL profile, your own profile (unlocks, achievements, scores) is
set aside while you play that career. Switch back to Sandbox Mode before removing the station and it is put back
for you. If you didn't, it is still in FTL's saves folder, at FederationHomePlanet\ftl-profile\ae_prof.sav
(prof.sav without the Advanced Edition). To restore it, close FTL, move FTL's current ae_prof.sav out of the
saves folder, and put that one in its place.


NEW VERSIONS

In Settings, on the About tab, Check for Updates opens the station's page on Nexus Mods, where the newest
version is. Unzip the new one anywhere: it finds your fleets by itself.
