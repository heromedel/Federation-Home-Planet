# Lore components found

Real FTL lore, one fact a line, each with its source: FTL's own text (quoted), or heromedel's knowledge of the game
where the text doesn't show it. Every system, outcome and message is checked against this file (CLAUDE.md, the third
hard rule). It is edited only with heromedel's permission: new components are offered to him as a numbered list first.

## Races

1. Rock are immune to fire. *(FTL: "Immune to fire")*
2. A Lanius drains oxygen from the room it is in. *(FTL: "Drains oxygen from rooms.")*
3. A Lanius takes no damage from lack of oxygen. *(FTL: "Slow movement but no damage from lack of oxygen.")*
4. A fire goes out without oxygen. *(FTL tutorial: "Opening doors allows you to drain sections of your ship of oxygen
   and put out fires.")*
5. A Zoltan powers the system in the room it is in. *(FTL: "Provides power to occupied system")*
6. Slugs' telepathy shows where lifeforms are, which room each one is in, even with sensors down; it does not show the
   room itself (no hull breaches, fires or system states). Slugs are immune to mind control. Assume no other abilities.
   *(FTL: "Telepathic powers reveal rooms and lifeforms even when sensors are down", "Immune to mind control."; what
   it shows: heromedel's play)*
7. It is unclear whether the Engi are partly organic or entirely mechanical. *(FTL: "It's unclear if the 'Engi' are
   partly organic or entirely mechanical")*
8. The Engi are exceptional engineers: repair speed doubled, combat damage halved. *(FTL: "they make exceptional
   engineers", "Repair speed is doubled", "Combat damage inflicted is halved")*
9. An Engi is made up of trillions of nano-machines. *(FTL event text: "Engi is made up of trillions of nano-machines")*
10. The Mantis are a vicious warrior race: 1.5 times the combat damage, half the repair speed. *(FTL: "a vicious warrior
    race", "Inflict 1.5x damage in combat", "Halved repair speed")*
11. The Rock have a reputation as fearsome pirates, with few diplomatic ties. *(FTL event text: "As a naturally warlike
    species with few intergalactic diplomatic ties, the Rock people have garnered quite a reputation as fearsome
    pirates.")*
12. The Crystal are the ancient ancestors of the Rock. *(FTL: "Ancient ancestors of the Rockmen.")*

## Hazards

13. A solar flare is a hazard a ship can run into at a beacon. *(heromedel)*
14. In vanilla FTL a beacon never has two different hazards, and a hazard beacon rarely has a planet. *(checked in all
    83 of FTL's hazard events: none has two kinds; about five show a planet)*
15. Solar flares start fires. *(FTL tooltip: "Solar flares will light the ship on fire. Shields will reduce the
    effect.")*
16. Solar flares cause hull damage. *(FTL tip: "Solar flares will cause hull damage in addition to starting fires.")*
17. In an asteroid field, asteroids periodically strike any ship there: a Zoltan shield first if there is one, then
    the normal shields, and the hull only if the shields are down. *(heromedel; FTL tooltip: "Periodically asteroids
    will strike your ship.")*
18. A pulsar sends periodic waves of electromagnetic energy that disrupt systems. *(FTL tooltip: "Periodic waves of
    electromagnetic energy will disrupt your systems.")*
19. A plasma storm holds the reactor at half power. *(FTL tooltip: "Your main reactor can only function at half
    capacity.")*
20. Inside a nebula, sensors don't work. *(FTL tooltip: "Your sensors will not function")*
21. Inside a nebula, the rebel fleet advances more slowly. *(FTL tooltip: "the Rebel fleet will advance more slowly
    towards you")*
22. An Anti-Ship Battery is on a planet, so it needs one at the beacon. *(FTL tooltips: "An Anti-Ship Battery on the
    planet is targeting you", "Planet-side anti-ship batteries are detected in this system.")*

## Systems and augments

23. In vanilla FTL a ship holds at most 8 systems; The Federation Home Planet has options to exceed this. *(FTL store
    text: "You've reached the System Limit"; heromedel)*
24. A Clone Bay clones dead crew members who were on that ship, or in a raiding party from it. *(heromedel)*
25. Cloning costs a loss of skill. *(FTL: "Automatically clones dead crew with skill penalty.")*
26. A Clone Bay must be powered at the time of death, unless the ship has a Backup DNA Bank. *(heromedel; FTL: "Your
    crew is safe in clone storage even if the system is off or broken.")*
27. A Clone Bay heals crew partially every jump, with no power needed. *(FTL: "crew heals partially every jump. Jump
    heal is passive and requires no power.")*
28. In vanilla FTL a Clone Bay replaces the Medbay: never both on one ship. *(heromedel)*
29. A Clone Bay put in a different room from the Medbay still removes the Medbay. *(heromedel, confirmed)*
30. Long-Ranged Scanners only show information about nearby beacons, one jump away. *(FTL: "Adds additional info about
    nearby Beacons on the star map.")*

## Travel

37. A jump is chosen by the captain and goes to a beacon on the map; a ship doesn't arrive somewhere it didn't choose.
    *(heromedel)*

## Slugs and their nebulas

38. Every Slug ship carries Slug Repair Gel, which automatically repairs hull breaches. No Slug ship starts with a
    Backup Battery. *(FTL: "Slug ships excrete a thick gel that automatically repairs any hull breaches."; FTL
    blueprints: no Slug ship starts with a battery, and the enemy Slug ships have no room for one)*
39. Slugs navigate the nebulas they live in by telepathy, and are reluctant to go beyond nebulas. *(FTL event text:
    "Today they use this ability to navigate unfettered the depths of the nebulas they inhabit.", "The Slugs rely
    heavily on their telepathic powers and are reluctant to give up that advantage by extending beyond nebulas.")*
40. The Slugs developed on an ocean planet, where sensing other organisms mattered more than sight. *(FTL event text:
    "The Slugs developed on an ocean planet where the ability to telepathically sense another organism was more
    important than sight.")*
41. The Slug ships players can fly have a sensors room. They start without Sensors installed, but Sensors can be
    bought. *(FTL blueprints: each of the three Slug cruisers lists sensors, not started; Type A: "Designed for use
    inside nebulas, this cruiser lacks sensors")*
42. Slug ships and storms: FTL contradicts itself, so neither is used as fact. *(FTL event text: "the largely unaffected
    Slug ships" in one, "Slugs generally avoid these storms" in another)*
43. FTL calls the nebula storm both a plasma storm and an ion storm. *(FTL event text: "a plasma storm" in most storm
    events, "the ion storm" in the Slug ones)*
44. The Mantis hunt Slugs in their own nebulas as a rare test of honour for their mightiest crews. *(FTL event text:
    "The Mantis attack ship here looks to have been hunting Slugs on their home turf - a rare test of honor for the
    mightiest Mantis crews.")*
45. The Slug Cruiser Type B is named The Stormwalker; nothing in its blueprint handles storms. *(FTL:
    "The Stormwalker")*

46. The Slugs are a leisure-centred civilisation that chases currency, which leads to treachery. *(FTL event text:
    "Everything in Slug life is done in the pursuit of more currency and more time in which to spend it on extravagant
    ventures. This, inevitably, leads to much treachery in open space.")*

## heromedel's extrapolations

Not vanilla FTL: heromedel's own reading of the game, kept as he wrote it. The station's lore follows it like the
facts above.

50. Not a vanilla fact and is more than one point but will be kept together. Hero's extrapolation: Slugs sense other
    lifeforms and thus when ships are near by and possibly ion storms in nebulas but they cannot sense far away enough
    to tell if adjacent beacons have ships or ion storms. When they send Ship Models to The Federation, they have room
    for Sensors to be installed for use by other races. The Slug Ships ability to survive based in nebulas has to do
    with Slug Repair gel not resistance to the storms that they avoid. Mantis like hunting in nebulas as a challenge and
    because they hate how slugs see them coming but they still are not good at it.
