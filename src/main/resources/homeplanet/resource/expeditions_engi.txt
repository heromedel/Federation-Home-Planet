# Expeditions: the Engi Controlled Sector. Networks, protocols and machines that work too well; the Engi are
# courteous, collective and poor fighters. (Format: see expeditions.txt.)

event engi_network engi,engi_home low network
The Engi relay's network has split into a dozen parts, each certain the others are wrong. The station's Engi drift between consoles, chattering in distress.
* Carry replacement components
  ok scrap 6-10 | Hard work, but the network begins to agree with itself again. The Engi pay in scrap and polite whirring.
* Offer to debug the network
  roll 40
  win scrap 12-16 parts 2 | Against every expectation, the patch holds. The Engi give spare drone parts as thanks.
  lose scrap 2-4 | The patch makes things worse before the Engi undo it. They pay a little, for effort.
* [engi] {who} joins the network
  ok scrap 14-20 parts 2 | {who} plugs in and the dozen parts become one again. The Engi are grateful, in their quiet way.

event drone_factory engi moderate factory
The drone factory's doors open on their own as your crew arrives. Inside, the assembly lines are still running, building drones no one has ordered, for an owner who stopped answering a year ago.
* Shut down the main power
  roll 55
  win scrap 10-14 then line | The lines grind to a halt. At the end of one, a finished drone sits waiting.
  lose injure 1 scrap 4-6 | A guard drone wakes as the lights go down. {who} is hurt before they find its off switch.
* Take what's near the door and leave
  ok scrap 5-8 | Your crew takes what it can carry and leaves the factory to its work.
* [engi] {who} asks the factory what it wants
  ok scrap 6-8 then line | {who} listens at a terminal for a long while. "It is lonely," {who} reports. "It will give us something if we stay to talk."
* [red human] {who} wants to sell the factory's location
  roll 40
  win scrap 18-24 | A broker pays well for the coordinates. The Engi may not be pleased, if they ever find out.
  lose end | The Engi find out before your crew leaves. They are escorted home, politely, with nothing.
step line
The finished drone hums on its cradle, its targeting light blinking patiently.
* Take it
  roll 70
  win item drone | It powers down for transport without complaint.
  lose injure 1 | Its targeting light settles on {who}, and it fires once before it shuts down.
* Leave it on the line
  ok scrap 4-6 | Your crew strips the line's spare parts instead, and leaves the drone where it is.

event data_core engi moderate courier
The Engi archivists hand over a data core the size of a fist and a route that avoids every relay in the sector. Two beacons out, someone starts asking for it on an open channel.
* Keep quiet and keep moving
  roll 60
  win scrap 12-16 | The core reaches its destination. The archivists pay, and say nothing about what was on it.
  lose injure 1 scrap 6-8 | A boarding party catches the shuttle at the second beacon. {who} is hurt before they're driven off.
* Answer and ask what they want
  roll 45
  win scrap 8-10 then offer | The voice belongs to an Engi who says the core holds something that should never be read.
  lose end | It was a trace. The shuttle is surrounded by the next beacon, and your crew hands the core over to save their skins.
* [engi] {who} encrypts the core a second time
  ok scrap 14-18 | Whatever is on it, nobody will be reading it now. The archivists are impressed.
step offer
"Give it to me," the Engi says, "and I will pay more than the archive. Or deliver it, and live with what happens."
* Sell it to the stranger
  ok scrap 18-24 | The stranger pays and is gone. The archivists send a single message afterwards: "Noted."
* Deliver it as agreed
  ok scrap 12-14 | The archivists take it without a word, and pay exactly what they promised.

event hive_split engi moderate refugees
A cluster of Engi has broken away from its network after a disagreement nobody outside it can follow. They have no ship, no protocol and no idea what to do next.
* Ferry them to a neutral station
  ok scrap 6-10 | The Engi are polite, unhappy and silent all the way. At the station they pay you in parts.
* Return them to their network
  roll 60
  win scrap 10-14 | The network takes them back, and pays for the trouble.
  lose injure 1 | The network does not want them back, and makes that clear with its turrets. {who} is hit.
* [human] {who} offers them work on the shuttle
  ok join engi | One of them decides that a single human's protocol is better than none. It signs on.
* [slug] {who} reads what the cluster disagreed about
  ok scrap 8-12 | It was whether outsiders can be trusted. {who} tells the cluster what it's thinking, and it pays to stop being reminded.

event repair_dock engi low repair
An Engi repair dock offers free work to any ship that brings its own crew. The catch, the dockmaster explains, is that the crew must be repaired too.
* Agree to the checkup
  roll 75
  win scrap 2-4 | Your crew is scanned, prodded and declared functional. The dock pays them for the data.
  lose injure 1 | The scanner is not calibrated for organics. {who} is the one who finds out.
* Decline and move on
  ok | The dockmaster is disappointed, and says so twice.
* [engi] {who} vouches for the crew
  ok scrap 6-8 parts 2 | {who} convinces the dock that the crew is in excellent working order. The dock pays for the referral.

event engi_protocol engi high protocol
An Engi patrol stops the shuttle and recites a long protocol about unregistered biological cargo. By "cargo" they mean your crew.
* Submit to quarantine
  roll 60
  win scrap 4-6 | Two days in a sterile room, then release and an apology. The Engi pay compensation exactly to the credit.
  lose injure 1 | The decontamination is harsh. {who} comes out with burns.
* Make a break for it
  roll 40
  win scrap 6-10 | The shuttle slips away. Engi patrols are slow to decide to shoot.
  lose lose 1 | They decide faster than expected. The shuttle limps away without {who}.
* [engi] {who} files the correct exemption
  ok scrap 10-14 | {who} submits the form before the patrol has finished reciting. The patrol apologises, and pays for the delay.
* [zoltan] {who} invokes interspecies treaty law
  ok scrap 8-10 | The patrol checks the treaty, finds {who} is right, and lets the shuttle go with a formal apology.

event broken_construct engi low salvage
An Engi construction frame drifts at the beacon, abandoned half-built, its arms still flexing in slow loops.
* Strip its outer plates
  ok scrap 6-10 | The frame keeps flexing as your crew works. It doesn't seem to mind.
* Cut into its core for the processor
  roll 55
  win scrap 12-16 parts 2 | The processor is intact, and worth a great deal.
  lose injure 1 | The frame's arms finally take notice. {who} is caught in one.
* [engi] {who} tells it to stop
  ok scrap 10-14 | {who} sends a single command. The frame folds itself up neatly for transport.
