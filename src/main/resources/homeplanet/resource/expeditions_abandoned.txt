# Expeditions: the Abandoned Sector. Dead stations, empty colonies and the Lanius who eat what's left; airless, quiet
# and not as empty as it looks. (Format: see expeditions.txt.)

event lanius_station abandoned moderate lanius
The dead station is full of Lanius, and the Lanius are full of the dead station: they've been eating its metal for weeks.
* Salvage the parts they haven't reached yet
  roll 55
  win scrap 10-16 | Your crew works fast and comes away with good salvage.
  lose injure 1 scrap 4-6 | The Lanius notice. {who} gets out with the salvage and a bad burn from the cold.
* Trade with the Lanius
  roll 50
  win scrap 8-12 parts 2 | The Lanius trade what they don't want: scrap, and old drone parts.
  lose end | The Lanius don't trade. Your crew is escorted to the airlock, and home.
* [lanius] {who} speaks with them
  ok scrap 14-18 parts 2 | {who} and the Lanius understand each other. The salvage is shared fairly.
* [engi] {who} offers the Lanius a broken drone to eat
  ok scrap 10-14 | The Lanius accept the drone with something like delight, and let your crew take what they like.

event colony_survey abandoned low survey
The abandoned colony is quiet, and the quiet is wrong: no wind, no machines, no air.
* Survey it in suits, carefully
  ok scrap 6-10 | The survey is slow and safe. The colony's records sell well.
* Search the colony's vault
  roll 45
  win scrap 12-18 item augment | The vault holds the colony's last valuables, and a piece of ship gear.
  lose injure 1 | The last colonists trapped the vault. {who} is hurt.
* [lanius] {who} goes in without a suit
  ok scrap 12-16 | {who} works through the airless colony quickly and finds what everyone else missed.

event mining_fleet abandoned moderate fleet
The missing mining fleet is here: nine ships, docked in a ring around a dead asteroid, every hull eaten down to the frame.
* Search the flagship for its log
  roll 55
  win scrap 8-12 then log | The log survived in the flagship's black box.
  lose injure 1 | The frame gives way as {who} climbs through it.
* Photograph the ring and report back
  ok scrap 6-8 | The insurers pay for the pictures, and close the file.
* [lanius] {who} asks the asteroid's Lanius what happened
  ok scrap 10-14 then log | The Lanius were invited, {who} learns. Someone sold the fleet to them.
step log
The log ends with the fleet's captain accepting a contract to mine here, signed by a company the insurers also cover.
* Give the insurers everything
  ok scrap 14-18 | The insurers pay well, and a company's lawyers will be busy for years.
* Sell the log to the company instead
  ok scrap 18-24 | The company pays more, and quickly. Your crew doesn't sleep well for a while.

event last_light abandoned low beacon
A single light burns in the window of an otherwise dark station. Inside, an automated voice is still reading the evening news from seven years ago.
* Switch it off
  ok scrap 4-6 | The station falls silent. Your crew strips its kitchen for scrap.
* Listen to the end of the broadcast
  ok scrap 2-4 | The news is old, and the weather was fine. At the end, the voice wishes everyone a good night. Nobody aboard laughs.
* [engi] {who} copies the station's records
  ok scrap 8-12 | {who} saves seven years of station logs. A historian will pay for them, eventually.

event metal_plague abandoned high plague
The Lanius in this system aren't eating metal. They're sick: their bodies flake and crumble, and the healthy ones keep their distance.
* Bring medical supplies from the nearest station
  roll 55
  win scrap 12-16 | The supplies help, a little. The Lanius pay in the only currency they have: salvage.
  lose injure 1 | One of the sick Lanius, desperate, grabs {who} for their metal. It hurts.
* Leave quickly
  ok | Your crew leaves the system and the sick behind. Nobody aboard says a word for a long time.
* [lanius] {who} helps the healthy ones care for the sick
  roll 70
  win scrap 10-14 join lanius | {who} stays three days. When your crew leaves, one of the recovered goes with them.
  lose lose 1 | {who} catches it. The Lanius take {who} in, and won't give them back.

event drifting_tram abandoned moderate tram
A tram still runs on the dead colony's orbital ring, stopping at empty stations, opening its doors, closing them, moving on.
* Ride it to the end of the line
  roll 60
  win scrap 8-12 then terminus | The last station is still lit.
  lose injure 1 | The tram brakes hard at a broken section. {who} is thrown against the doors.
* Strip the nearest station
  ok scrap 6-8 | The station's machines sell well enough.
* [human] {who} finds the line's control room
  ok scrap 10-14 | {who} stops the tram, and finds its maintenance stores full of parts.
step terminus
At the last station, a family of colonists has been living on the tram's power for seven years. They're thin, wary, and very polite.
* Offer them a ride out
  ok scrap 4-6 join any | Most of them stay; the station is home. One comes with your crew.
* Trade with them and leave them be
  ok scrap 10-14 | They trade old colony scrap for news. They don't want to leave.

event scavenger_rivals abandoned high scavengers
Your crew isn't the only salvage team at the wreck. The other one has guns, and has already decided the wreck is theirs.
* [fight] Fight them for it
  roll 40
  win scrap 16-22 item weapon | The other team leaves faster than it came, and leaves its gear.
  lose lose 1 | The other team was better armed. {who} doesn't come back from the wreck.
* Split the wreck with them
  roll 70
  win scrap 8-12 | The split is fair. Both teams leave with something.
  lose injure 1 | The other team keeps its half and takes a shot at your crew on the way out. {who} is hit.
* Let them have it
  ok | Your crew leaves the wreck to them. There are other wrecks.
