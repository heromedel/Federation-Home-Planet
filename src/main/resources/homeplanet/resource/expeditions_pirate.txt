# Expeditions: the Pirate Controlled Sector. Greed, gangs and markets that sell anything; pirates of every race,
# loyal to scrap and little else. (Format: see expeditions.txt.)

event asteroid_criminals pirate high hideout
The rebel criminals have dug into an asteroid riddled with tunnels. Their sentries are bored and badly placed.
* Slip past the sentries
  roll 50
  win scrap 14-20 | Your crew takes the gang's leader without a shot fired. The bounty is paid in full.
  lose injure 1 scrap 6-8 then tunnels | A sentry spots them. {who} is wounded, and the gang scatters into the tunnels.
* [fight] Hit the front entrance hard
  roll 40
  win scrap 18-24 item weapon | The gang surrenders after a short, loud fight. Their armoury pays for the trip twice over.
  lose lose 1 | The gang was ready. Your crew retreats without {who}.
* [lanius] {who} drains the air from their tunnels
  ok scrap 16-22 | {who} walks through the vacuum, unbothered. The gang surrenders, gasping.
step tunnels
The tunnels branch three ways, and every one of them is dark.
* Split up and search
  roll 40
  win scrap 12-16 | {crew} finds the leader hiding in a supply locker.
  lose lose 1 | Splitting up was a mistake. {who} is never found.
* Seal the tunnels and wait
  roll 65
  win scrap 10-14 | Two days later, the gang comes out with its hands up, hungry.
  lose end | They had another way out. The bounty goes unpaid.
* [slug] {who} feels for their thoughts
  ok scrap 12-16 | {who} points down the left tunnel without a word. The leader is there.

event ransom pirate moderate ransom
The pirate den is loud, crowded and dangerous. The ransom is in a small case, and every pirate here knows it.
* Deliver the ransom and walk out
  roll 65
  win scrap 10-14 | The hostages are freed. Their families pay your crew for their courage.
  lose injure 1 scrap 4-6 | A pirate tries for the case on the way out. {who} keeps it, and gets hurt.
* [fight] Keep the ransom and free the hostages by force
  roll 35
  win scrap 20-26 | The pirates never saw it coming. The hostages are freed, and the ransom comes home too.
  lose lose 1 | The pirates were ready. The hostages are freed, but {who} pays the price.
* [mantis] {who} speaks to the pirate captain
  ok scrap 12-16 | The captain is Mantis too. After a long exchange, the ransom is halved, and your crew keeps the difference.

event medical_market pirate moderate market
The stolen medical supplies are on a market stall, sold by a pirate with a missing eye and a very large friend.
* Buy them back
  ok scrap 4-6 | It costs most of the reward, but the clinic gets its supplies back and adds a little for the trouble.
* [fight] Take them back
  roll 45
  win scrap 12-16 | The large friend is slower than he looks. Your crew walks out with the supplies.
  lose injure 1 | The large friend is exactly as fast as he looks. {who} wakes up outside.
* Find out who sold them to the stall
  roll 50
  win scrap 8-10 then source | The stallholder, under some pressure, names a supplier.
  lose | The stallholder has never heard of any supplier, and has never seen your crew before either.
step source
The supplier is the clinic's own quartermaster, living well on a pirate moon.
* Bring him back to the clinic
  roll 55
  win scrap 12-16 | The clinic pays for the supplies and the quartermaster. He doesn't stop talking the whole way.
  lose injure 1 | The quartermaster has friends. {who} is cut in the scuffle, and he escapes.
* Sell the clinic his name and leave it to them
  ok scrap 8-10 | The clinic pays, grimly. What they do with the name is their own business.

event card_shark pirate low gambling
A pirate gambling den offers a seat at its table. The dealer is a Slug, and smiles at your crew's cards before they do.
* Play a few hands
  roll 30
  win scrap 12-18 | Against every law of the den, {crew} wins. The dealer looks very, very interested.
  lose | The dealer wins every hand that matters. At least the stakes were low.
  aside 30 | On the way out, the dealer winks at {crew}. Slugs don't have eyelids.
* Watch from the bar and leave
  ok scrap 2-3 | {crew} listens at the bar instead, and sells a rumour on the way out.
* [slug] {who} takes the dealer's seat
  ok scrap 14-20 | Two Slugs, one table. The other players leave. {who} and the dealer split the pot, smiling.

event gang_war pirate high gangs
Two pirate gangs are about to settle a dispute at the beacon, and both want to hire your crew. Neither wants to hear that your crew is hired by the other.
* Side with the bigger gang
  roll 60
  win scrap 12-16 | The bigger gang wins. They pay, and promise not to remember your crew's faces.
  lose injure 1 | The smaller gang had friends. {who} is caught in the crossfire.
* Side with the smaller gang
  roll 35
  win scrap 20-26 item weapon | The smaller gang wins. They pay double, and throw in a captured gun.
  lose lose 1 | The smaller gang loses. So does {who}.
* Take both their advances and leave before it starts
  roll 50
  win scrap 14-18 | Your crew is three beacons away when the shooting starts.
  lose injure 2 | Both gangs notice at the same time.

event deserter pirate low deserter

* Give her the ride
  ok scrap 8-12 | Her information is good. The Federation pays for it, and for her.
  aside 20 | A month later she sends word from a Federation academy. She's top of her class in gunnery.
* Sell her back to her captain
  ok scrap 12-16 | The captain pays well. Your crew doesn't ask what happens to her.
* [human] {who} offers her a place on the crew
  ok join human | She thinks about it for about a second, then shakes {who}'s hand.
* [slug] {who} checks whether she's telling the truth
  ok scrap 10-14 | She is. The Federation pays more for a source somebody has vouched for.
