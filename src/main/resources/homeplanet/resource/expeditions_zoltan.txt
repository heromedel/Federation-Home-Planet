# Expeditions: the Zoltan Controlled Sector. Light, law and patience; the Zoltan are polite, exact, slow to anger and
# very hard to deceive. (Format: see expeditions.txt.)

event zoltan_census zoltan low census
The Zoltan observers count everything: moons, rocks, ships, and your crew. They count your crew several times.
* Be counted patiently
  ok scrap 6-8 | The Zoltan approve of your crew's patience, and say so at length. They pay exactly what was promised.
  aside 20 | The Zoltan's final count includes {crew} twice, as two different species.
* Help count
  roll 60
  win scrap 8-12 | The Zoltan approve of your crew's precision.
  lose scrap 3-4 | {crew} loses count twice. The Zoltan are too polite to comment, but the pay is less.
* [zoltan] {who} speaks with the observers
  ok scrap 10-12 fuel 2 | {who} and the observers glow at each other for an hour. The Zoltan pay more, and add fuel.
* [engi] {who} counts faster than the Zoltan
  ok scrap 10-12 | {who} finishes the count before the observers, and is correct. The Zoltan are delighted to be beaten.

event anomaly zoltan,zoltan_home moderate anomaly
The energy readings near the relay don't match anything the Zoltan know. Neither does the light coming from the empty patch of space ahead.
* Fly closer and take readings
  roll 50
  win scrap 12-16 then source | The readings are worth a great deal. And the light seems to be coming from something solid.
  lose injure 1 | A pulse from the light burns through the shuttle's shielding. {who} is hurt.
* Report it and keep away
  ok scrap 3-5 | The Zoltan take the report seriously, and pay for it.
* [red rock] {who} wants to throw something at it
  roll 50
  win scrap 10-12 | The rock passes through, and the light goes out. The Zoltan don't know whether to be grateful.
  lose injure 1 | The light sends the rock straight back, faster. {who} is the one it hits.
step source
At the heart of the light is a sphere of metal no larger than a head, warm, and humming in a rhythm that sounds almost like speech.
* Bring it back for the Zoltan
  roll 65
  win scrap 14-20 | The Zoltan receive it with a reverence your crew doesn't understand, and pay generously.
  lose injure 1 scrap 6-8 | It burns through its container on the way. {who} catches it before it hits the deck.
* Leave it where it is
  ok scrap 4-6 | The Zoltan say that was wise. They don't say why.
* [zoltan] {who} answers the hum
  ok scrap 10-12 item augment | {who} hums back. The sphere falls silent, then opens, and inside is a device the Zoltan let your crew keep.

event tribunal zoltan low law
The Zoltan tribunal needs outside witnesses to a contract dispute between two mining families. The hearing is expected to take nine hours, and it does.
* Sit through it all
  ok scrap 8-10 | The verdict is fair, the pay is exact, and nobody remembers the first five hours.
  aside 25 | The tribunal's clerk gives each witness a certificate of attendance. It is nine pages long.
* Offer an opinion
  roll 50
  win scrap 10-14 | The tribunal finds your crew's opinion useful, and pays a consultant's fee.
  lose scrap 2-4 | Witnesses are not asked for opinions. The tribunal says so, and docks the fee.
* [human] {who} spots the lie in the testimony
  ok scrap 12-16 | {who} notices one family's ledger doesn't add up. The tribunal is impressed, and the other family very grateful.
* [slug] {who} listens to the testimony behind the testimony
  ok scrap 12-14 | {who} tells the tribunal which family is lying. The tribunal checks, and pays for the saving of a day.

event power_loan zoltan moderate power
A Zoltan freighter has lost its reactor and is running on the bodies of its crew, who are tiring fast. The captain asks whether your crew would carry a spare core over from the next beacon.
* Make the run
  roll 65
  win scrap 10-14 | The core arrives in time. The Zoltan captain glows brighter than anyone, and pays.
  lose injure 1 scrap 6-8 | A rough docking jars the core loose. {who} wrestles it back into its cradle, and is burned doing it.
* Take the crew off and leave the freighter
  ok scrap 4-6 | The Zoltan come aboard, dim and grateful. They leave the freighter to drift.
* [zoltan] {who} lends power to their reactor
  ok scrap 12-16 | {who} stands in the reactor room for six hours, keeping it alive until help arrives.

event isolation zoltan high border
The Zoltan border station refuses to let the shuttle through. Its keeper is courteous, firm, and has a cannon that tracks the shuttle as it talks.
* Turn back and go around
  ok end | The detour takes the rest of the job's time. Your crew heads home with nothing but the route.
* Run the border
  roll 40
  win scrap 14-20 | The cannon fires once, and misses. Your crew gets through and finishes the job.
  lose lose 1 | The cannon doesn't miss twice. The shuttle limps home without {who}.
* [zoltan] {who} requests passage in the old forms
  ok scrap 10-14 | The keeper recognises the forms, and is pleased to be asked properly. The border opens.
* [slug] {who} reads the keeper's orders
  ok scrap 10-14 | The keeper has orders to let through anyone on the Federation register. Your crew is on it.

event shield_test zoltan moderate testing
A Zoltan research platform is testing a new kind of shield and needs volunteers to stand on the other side of it while it's fired at.
* Volunteer
  roll 70
  win scrap 12-16 | The shield holds. The Zoltan are delighted, and pay a hazard rate.
  aside 20 | The Zoltan name the shield's next version after {crew}. The version after that is named after a moon.
  lose injure 1 scrap 6-8 | The shield holds, mostly. {who} was standing near the part that didn't.
* Offer to fire the guns instead
  ok scrap 6-8 | The Zoltan are happy to have the help, and pay a gunner's wage.
* [crystal] {who} offers to stand where the shield fails
  ok scrap 14-18 | {who}'s crystal takes the hits the shield doesn't. The Zoltan take long notes.

event dimming zoltan high illness
A Zoltan colony is dimming. Its people grow fainter by the week, and its doctors can find no cause. They ask your crew to fetch a specialist from the next system.
* Fetch the specialist
  roll 60
  win scrap 12-16 then cure | The specialist comes, and finds the cause in a day.
  lose injure 1 then cure | The specialist comes, but the route back is longer than the shuttle could manage. {who} is hurt in a rough jump.
* Try to find the cause yourselves
  roll 30
  win scrap 18-24 | {crew} finds it: the colony's reactor is draining them through the floor. The Zoltan pay everything they have.
  lose scrap 2-4 | Your crew finds nothing. The colony thanks them for trying.
step cure
The cure needs something the Zoltan don't have: a lot of living energy, from someone outside the colony.
* Let the specialist take what she needs from the shuttle's reactor
  ok scrap 8-10 | The reactor runs cold for a week. The colony glows again.
* [zoltan] {who} gives their own light
  roll 75
  win scrap 14-18 | {who} lies in the infirmary for two days, faint but alive. The colony names a light after them.
  lose lose 1 | {who} gives too much. The colony glows again, and its people mourn a stranger.
* Leave the cure to the specialist and go home
  ok scrap 4-6 | The specialist says she'll find another way. Your crew hopes she does.

event impounded zoltan moderate award
The tribunal rules for your crew's testimony, and awards the witnesses a share of the forfeit: an impounded cruiser, her shield generator held back as evidence in another case.
* Accept the cruiser
  ok ship sector:limping:shields | She is towed out of the impound yard with great ceremony and no shields at all.
* Accept a cash settlement instead
  ok scrap 10-14 | The tribunal pays the settlement exactly, down to the last scrap.
* [zoltan] {who} petitions for the generator too
  roll 60
  win ship sector:towed | The tribunal debates for an hour, then agrees: the evidence is no longer needed. She comes whole.
  lose ship sector:limping:shields | The petition is denied, politely and at length. The cruiser comes without her shields.
