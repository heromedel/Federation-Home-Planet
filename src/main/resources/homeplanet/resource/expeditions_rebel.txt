# Expeditions: the Rebel Controlled Sector. Patrols, checkpoints, automated scouts and frightened people; the rebels
# hold this space, and Federation crew are not welcome in it. (Format: see expeditions.txt.)

event patrol rebel moderate patrol
A rebel patrol stops the transport. They want to see papers, and they are looking for an excuse not to believe them.
* Show the forged papers
  roll 60
  win scrap 6-10 | The papers pass. Your crew reaches the Federation's contact on time.
  lose injure 1 end | The papers fail. Your crew escapes, and {who} is hurt in the attempt.
* Hide in the cargo hold
  roll 65
  win scrap 6-8 | The rebels search the passenger deck and leave.
  lose lose 1 | The rebels search the hold. {who} doesn't make it out.
* [human] {who} talks their way through
  ok scrap 10-14 | {who} convinces the rebels they're traders, and even sells them some old maps.
  aside 20 | The maps {who} sold them are eleven years out of date.

event intel_contact rebel high intelligence
The Federation contact is an old woman running a supply depot behind rebel lines. She has the intelligence, and she wants to leave with it.
* Take her with you
  roll 55
  win scrap 14-20 then pursuit | She makes it to the shuttle. So does a rebel patrol, two minutes behind.
  lose injure 1 scrap 8-10 | A patrol spots them on the way out. {who} is wounded covering her escape.
* Take only the intelligence
  ok scrap 8-12 | She stays behind and wishes your crew well. The intelligence is good.
* [zoltan] {who} masks the escape
  ok scrap 14-18 | {who} bends the depot's lights to hide them. The rebels never see them leave.
step pursuit
The patrol is gaining. The shuttle can outrun it, or hide in the asteroid field ahead.
* Run for it
  roll 50
  win scrap 24-28 | The shuttle is faster, just. The contact laughs all the way home.
  lose injure 1 | A shot clips the engines. {who} is hurt keeping them running.
* Hide in the asteroids
  roll 65
  win scrap 4-6 join human | The patrol passes by. The contact, grateful, asks to join the crew that saved her.
  lose lose 1 | The patrol searches the field. {who} draws them away from the others, and doesn't come back.

event downed_pilot rebel high rescue
The downed pilot is hiding in the ruins of a mining town on a rebel-held moon. Her beacon is still on. So is the rebel garrison's.
* Go in at night
  roll 50
  win scrap 12-16 then pilot | The garrison sleeps. The pilot's hiding place is where the beacon says.
  lose lose 1 | The garrison doesn't sleep. {who} draws them off so the others can escape.
* Signal her to come to the shuttle
  roll 40
  win scrap 10-14 join human | She makes it across the open ground, and asks to stay on with the crew that came for her.
  lose end | The signal brings the garrison instead. Your crew lifts off without her.
* [lanius] {who} crosses the airless plain
  ok scrap 12-16 then pilot | {who} walks across the moon's surface where no patrol thinks to look.
step pilot
The pilot is badly hurt, and the shuttle is a long walk away.
* Carry her
  roll 60
  win join human | It's slow, but they make it. She signs on as soon as she can stand.
  lose injure 1 | A patrol spots them. {who} is hit, but they make it to the shuttle with her.
* Leave her the medkit and come back later
  ok scrap 6-8 | When your crew comes back, she's gone. The Federation pays for the attempt.

  roll 50
  win scrap 34-40 | The data is worth a great deal to Federation intelligence.

  lose injure 1 | The self-destruct is intact. {who} is caught in the blast.
* Strip it for parts and leave the data
  ok scrap 6-8 parts 2 | Your crew takes what it can carry and leaves the rest.
* [engi] {who} disarms it first
  ok scrap 14-18 parts 2 | {who} finds the self-destruct and switches it off. Then they take everything.
* [zoltan] {who} drains the scout's power before anyone touches it
  ok scrap 12-16 | With its batteries flat, the self-destruct is just a box. Your crew takes the data and the parts.
