# Expeditions: the Mantis Controlled Sector. Raiders, hunters and honour: the Mantis fight well, respect strength
# and despise hesitation. (Format: see expeditions.txt.)

event mantis_raid mantis,mantis_home high raid
A Mantis raiding party drops onto the convoy's lead ship. Your crew is hired to keep them off the others.
* [fight] Hold the airlocks
  roll 45
  win scrap 14-20 | The raiders break against the airlocks and withdraw. The convoy's captains pay well.
  lose injure 1 scrap 6-8 then breach | The airlocks hold until one doesn't. {who} is hurt as the raiders pour through.
* Seal the convoy's other ships and abandon the lead
  ok scrap 6-8 | The lead ship is lost to the raiders, but the rest are safe. The pay is cut for it.
* [mantis] {who} challenges their leader
  roll 70
  win scrap 18-24 | {who} wins the challenge. The raiders leave with honour, and the convoy pays a hero's wage.
  lose lose 1 | {who} fights well and loses. The raiders leave with their prize.
step breach
The raiders are inside the second ship, cutting toward its crew quarters.
* [fight] Go in after them
  roll 45
  win scrap 12-16 | Your crew catches them in the corridor. None of the raiders leave.
  lose lose 1 | It's a narrow corridor and the Mantis are faster. {who} falls holding it.
* Vent the corridor to space
  ok scrap 8-10 | The raiders, and the corridor, are gone. The crew quarters are safe. The captain doesn't say thank you.

event border_post mantis moderate guard
The trading post on the Mantis border is restless tonight. One of its Mantis customers insists the post's prices are an insult.
* Stand guard and stay calm
  roll 65
  win scrap 8-12 | The night passes with nothing worse than raised voices. The post pays in full.
  lose injure 1 scrap 6-8 | One customer loses patience. {who} stops them, and is cut doing it.
* Lower the prices for the night
  ok scrap 4-6 | The customers calm down, and so do the post's profits. The pay is smaller.
* [red mantis] {who} agrees the prices are an insult
  roll 30
  win scrap 12-16 | The Mantis customers approve of {who}, and pay the post's prices out of respect.
  lose injure 2 | The argument becomes a brawl, and your crew is in the middle of it.

event drifted_freighter mantis high recovery
The freighter drifted into Mantis space three weeks ago. When your crew finds her, her airlocks are open and her cargo bay is full of Mantis eggs.
* Leave the eggs and take the freighter
  roll 50
  win scrap 14-20 | Your crew tows the freighter out before anyone comes back for the eggs.
  lose injure 1 then hatchery | Someone was guarding them. {who} is hurt in the first moments.
* Destroy the eggs
  roll 40
  win scrap 12-16 | The eggs burn. Nothing comes for them, this time.
  lose lose 1 then hatchery | Something comes for them. {who} doesn't see it until it is far too late.
* [mantis] {who} speaks to the eggs' guardian
  ok scrap 16-22 | {who} finds the guardian, and they speak for a long time. The guardian lets the freighter go, the eggs moved to a safer place.
step hatchery
A Mantis guardian stands between your crew and the airlock, very still.
* [fight] Fight your way past
  roll 40
  win scrap 8-12 | The guardian falls. Your crew leaves quickly, and doesn't look back.
  lose injure 1 end | The guardian lets them go, bleeding, without the freighter.
* Back away slowly and leave
  ok end | The guardian watches them go. The freighter stays with the eggs.

event duel_offer mantis moderate duel
A Mantis warrior blocks the dock and offers a duel to any of your crew. The prize, she says, is her ship's cargo. The cost is obvious.
* Decline politely
  ok | She spits on the dock, says something about cowards, and lets your crew pass.
* [fight] Accept the duel
  roll 45
  win scrap 14-20 item weapon | {crew} wins, barely. She hands over the cargo with a deep, formal bow.
  lose injure 1 | {crew} loses, but she spares them, and says they fought well.
* [mantis] {who} accepts
  roll 75
  win scrap 18-24 item weapon | {who} wins. The two of them part as something close to friends.
  lose lose 1 | {who} loses, and the Mantis don't spare their own.

event hunting_trophy mantis low trophy

* Decline
  roll 70
  win | He is offended, but only a little.
  lose injure 1 | He is offended a great deal. {who} is the closest thing to hit.

  ok scrap 8-12 | It's a long story, and {who} listens to all of it. He gives them the skull for free.

event mantis_prisoners mantis moderate prisoners
A Mantis cruiser holds three human prisoners and offers them for sale. "Workers," the captain says. "Fed, mostly."
* Buy their freedom
  ok join human | Two of them go home. The third has nowhere to go, and signs on.
* Refuse, and report the cruiser
  ok scrap 4-6 | The Federation pays for the report, and promises to look into it.
* [fight] Free them by force
  roll 35
  win scrap 10-14 join human | The cruiser's crew was smaller than it looked. All three are free, and one signs on.
  lose lose 1 | The cruiser's crew was exactly as large as it looked. {who} becomes the fourth prisoner.

* [mantis] {who} keeps watch
