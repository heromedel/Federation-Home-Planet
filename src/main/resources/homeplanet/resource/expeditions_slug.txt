# Expeditions: the Slug Controlled Nebula. Deals, debts and lies; the Slug read minds, smile a great deal and are
# never quite what they seem. (Format: see expeditions.txt.)

event sealed_package slug,slug_home moderate package
The sealed package hums faintly. The Slug who handed it over said not to open it, which is exactly what a Slug would say.
* Deliver it unopened
  roll 65
  win scrap 12-16 | The package is delivered. The recipient pays, and smiles too much.
  lose injure 1 | The package opens itself on the way. Whatever was inside bites {who} and escapes into the vents.
* Open it and see
  roll 40
  win scrap 6-8 item augment | Inside is a piece of ship gear, worth more than the delivery. The Slug never ask about it.
  lose end | The package holds nothing at all. The delivery was a test, and your crew has failed it.
* [slug] {who} reads the sender's intent
  ok scrap 14-18 | {who} reads the truth: the delivery is honest, and the fee could be higher. It is.

event contract slug low contract
The Slug merchant's contract is fifty pages long, in very small print, and must be witnessed by outsiders.
* Read it all before signing as witnesses
  ok scrap 6-10 | It takes a full day, but your crew finds nothing wrong. The merchant pays, looking slightly disappointed.
  aside 20 | {crew} finds a recipe for fried fungus on page thirty. It is surprisingly good.
* Sign without reading
  roll 50
  win scrap 18-22 | Nothing goes wrong. The merchant pays.
  lose end | Page forty-two made the witnesses responsible for the merchant's debts. It takes the rest of the trip to get out of it.
* [human] {who} reads the fine print
  ok scrap 12-16 | {who} finds the trap on page forty-two and negotiates a better fee for keeping quiet about it.

event debt slug high debt
The debtor is a Slug, and so is the creditor, and both are sure the other is lying. Your crew has been hired to collect, and the debtor has invited them to dinner.
* Go to dinner
  roll 50
  win scrap 6-10 then dinner | Dinner is surprisingly good, and the debtor is surprisingly friendly.
  lose injure 1 | Dinner is poisoned. {who} eats first.
* Seize the debtor's cargo instead
  roll 40
  win scrap 16-22 | The cargo covers the debt with plenty over. The creditor is delighted.
  lose lose 1 | The debtor's guards were expecting this. {who} doesn't make it back to the shuttle.
* [slug] {who} reads both of them
  ok scrap 14-20 | {who} finds both of them are lying, and about the same amount. The debt is split, and so is the fee.
step dinner
Over the last course, the debtor makes an offer: "Tell my creditor I paid already, and I will pay you twice what he would."
* Take the offer
  ok scrap 18-24 | The debtor pays. The creditor finds out a week later, and your crew is never hired in the nebula again.
* Refuse, and demand the debt
  roll 55
  win scrap 32-38 | The debtor sighs and pays. "Honest," the Slug says, as if it were an illness.
  lose injure 1 | The debtor stops smiling. {who} is the first to find out how much.

event mind_market slug moderate memories
A Slug market sells memories: someone else's first kiss, a war, a sunset on a world that no longer exists. The stallholder offers to buy one of your crew's.
* Sell a harmless memory
  ok scrap 8-12 | {crew} sells the memory of a long, boring shuttle flight. The stallholder is delighted.
  aside 25 | Somewhere in the market, someone is enjoying a very boring shuttle flight.
* Buy a memory instead
  roll 50
  win scrap 10-14 fuel 3 | It's a smuggler's memory, and it includes the location of a fuel cache.
  lose injure 1 | It's a memory of drowning. {who} wakes up gasping, and doesn't sleep for a week.
* [slug] {who} haggles in thought
  ok scrap 14-18 | {who} and the stallholder trade thoughts in silence. Your crew comes out of it richer, and nobody knows how.

event nebula_lure slug,nebula moderate lure
The nebula's static plays tricks on the instruments. A voice on the comm claims to be the shuttle's own pilot, calling from outside.
* Ignore the voice and hold course
  roll 65
  win scrap 6-8 | The voice fades. Your crew reaches the destination unharmed.
  lose injure 1 | The shuttle runs into something the instruments didn't show. {who} is thrown against a bulkhead.
* Answer the voice
  roll 40
  win scrap 10-14 fuel 2 | The voice is a stranded freighter crew, bored and playing games. They pay for rescue in fuel and scrap.
  lose end | It was a Slug lure. By the time your crew realises, they've been led far off course and must turn for home.
* [slug] {who} recognises a Slug lure
  ok scrap 10-14 | {who} plays the trick back on the trickster, and the Slug pay to be left alone.

event slug_auction slug moderate auction
A Slug auctioneer is selling a ship's salvage, and every bidder in the room seems to know what everyone else is about to bid.
* Bid honestly
  roll 40
  win item weapon | Your crew's bid wins, to everyone's surprise. The salvage includes a weapon.
  lose scrap 2-4 | They're outbid at every turn. The auctioneer pays a small fee for their attendance.
* Don't bid; watch and learn
  ok scrap 4-6 | {crew} sells the list of who bid what to a buyer outside.
* [slug] {who} bids with their mind closed
  ok scrap 6-10 item drone | {who} keeps their thoughts shut tight and wins the best lot.

event hostage_trade slug high hostage
A Slug family has taken a human trader hostage over an unpaid bill. They'll release her for a price, or for an hour of someone else's company.
* Pay the bill
  ok scrap 2-4 | The trader is released and pays your crew back, with a little extra for the trouble.
* Offer the hour
  roll 50
  win scrap 10-14 | It's a long hour of being read. {crew} comes out shaken but whole, with the trader.
  lose injure 1 | It's a very long hour. {who} comes out with a headache that lasts a week.
* [fight] Break her out
  roll 35
  win scrap 14-18 join human | The family scatters. The trader, amazed, signs on.
  lose lose 1 | The family doesn't scatter. They keep {who} as well.

event slug_bargain slug moderate bargain
A Slug dealer offers your crew a "fine cruiser, barely used" for nothing at all, only a small favour to be named later.
* Take the deal
  ok ship sector:limping:pilot | She's fine in every way except one: there's no piloting system aboard. The dealer never mentioned it. The tow is long and quiet.
* Refuse
  ok scrap 12-16 | The dealer is disappointed, and pays your crew a little for their time, for reasons of its own.
* [slug] {who} reads what the dealer is hiding
  ok scrap 4-6 ship sector:towed | {who} finds both the missing helm and the favour the dealer had in mind. The helm comes back. The favour doesn't.
