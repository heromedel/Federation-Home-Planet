# The words of an expedition report (expedition_type 2 in the cfg): the frame is fixed, these fill it in. Edit freely;
# no build is needed, the station reads this file as it is. A line is "<kind> <job> [<band>] | <words>". Where a job
# has several lines of a kind, one is picked at random, so the same job doesn't read the same twice.
#
#   event <job> | <what the crew did>          after "Due to events during the assignment the crew"; several per job
#   band <job> <band> | <phrase>               after a name: "Bob <phrase>." Bands: died, injured, failed, success, high, top
#                                              (top: "and brought back <item>" is added when a 20 found something)
#   hazard <hazard> | <sentence>               flare, asteroids, pulsar, storm
#   prize <job> <kind> | <sentence>            hijack ship, hijack part, salvage part, rescue recruit ({name}: the ship's
#                                              name and class, the part's system, or the recruit's name and race)
#   captured | <phrase>                        after a name, taken on Get Boarded (a ransom letter always follows)
#   infirmary | <phrase>                       after a name, badly hurt: in the infirmary a while
#   offer <sector> | <words>                   the card on the board for a sector; several per sector
#   shrug <hazard> <race> | <sentence>         after a crew member's line when their race shrugged the hazard off
#                                              (not for the dead, the taken, the infirmary or the injured)
#   band <job> injured cause | <phrase>        an injury that names a cause other than any hazard: drawn with the
#                                              others, and alone for someone hurt whose race shrugged the hazard off
#
# Marked lines: any key but an offer can end in a sector ("event defend zoltan | ..."), and that line joins the
# unmarked ones only in that sector. Captured lines are marked by who took them instead: slavers, pirates, rebels or
# mantis (the same captors who sign the ransom letter). In a line after a name, {he} {him} {his} become she, her, her
# for a woman ({He} {His} to begin a sentence). Every line finishes its thought: a reader should never be left
# asking "decide what?".
#
# Jobs: defend attack negotiate boarded rescue salvage scout repair transport lost escort capture board hijack infection spiders
# Sectors: civilian engi zoltan mantis pirate rebel rock nebula abandoned crystal
