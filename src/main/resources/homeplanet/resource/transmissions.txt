# Transmissions: the messages The Federation Home Planet sends (Immersive Notifications), and their rewards.
# Edit freely. Each message starts with "== key", then "from:", "subject:" and, if it carries one, "reward:" lines,
# a blank line, and the text. {rank} is the player's rank, {ship} a ship's class and layout.
#
# Rewards, comma separated (they go to the Cargo Hold): scrap N, fuel N, missiles N, parts N (drone parts),
# item ID (a weapon, drone or augment), crew RACE (a volunteer: human, engi, mantis, energy, rock, slug, crystal,
# anaerobic), system ID (a stored system, e.g. cloaking, battery, clonebay), and "choice A | B" to let the player pick.
# Keys: welcome, welcome:career (Career messages begun in Sandbox Mode), stipend ({period} and {amount} are filled in), empty (the free command), pleaded (the order after a
# plea for a new ship; {ship} is what it offers), withdrawn (that plea withdrawn: its order leaves the inbox), stranded or stranded:junkyard (no ship to command and no free command waiting; the second when
# a hull waits in the Junkyard), promo:1, promo:2, order:<base ship id>, ach:<achievement id>,
# rescue and reward (after a final victory: {ship} is her name, {value} her full value in scrap; also shown as a
# notice when Transmissions are off).
#
# Reply chains: a letter with "replies: words -> next-key 5-10 | other words -> other-key 3-5" gets a Reply button;
# the chosen reply sends next-key's letter a random 5 to 10 beacons later. "then: next-key 5-7" sends the next letter
# by itself, that many beacons after this one. "cost: scrap N" makes the reward's claim cost N scrap from the Cargo
# Hold. "action: derelict" delivers the Engi Restoration Collective's derelict to the Junkyard with the letter.
# chain:one-hull starts once per fleet, the first time the boarded ship comes out of a battle with one point of hull
# ({name} is her name).
# chain:repair-job starts once per fleet, after three ships that couldn't fly were made to fly again; "action:
# repair-job" delivers the Nightjar to the Junkyard. Its letters may use {class} (her model), {extra} (the bonus),
# {pay} (what was paid), {waiting} (what returning her pays now), {value} (her value, for the claims office), {taken} (what it took) and {paidline}.

== welcome
from: Home Planet Liaison
subject: Welcome to the Front

{rank},

I see you have volunteered to serve in the war against the rebellion.

The Federation Home Planet has approved you to begin building your fleet. Congratulations: your sign-on bonus of {start} scrap is already waiting in The Home Planet Station's Cargo Hold.

Just remember: once you have your first ship and set out, you will need to reach another beacon with a station to be able to trade with The Home Planet Station or your other ships.

Aside from your stipend, you will have to earn your way or fund your fleet yourself. It's not cheap.

Expect a message from Home Planet Shipyard about your first ship. My recommendation: find a way to acquire 200 scrap, and when things start looking bleak out there, get your ship to a station and plot a New Journey. It's the only way to keep the rebels off your tail.

The Fleet is stretched thin out there, but it looks after its own. Serve us well and you will be rewarded. Betray us and die with the rebels.

Watch this channel. The Federation will be in contact.

---

For the Federation, and all its worlds.
Until every beacon is free.
~ Home Planet Liaison
