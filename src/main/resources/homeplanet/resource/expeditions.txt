# Expeditions: the postings on the board, and the events the commander and crew meet on them.
#
# posting <kind> <the posting's words>       kinds: rescue, escort, salvage, survey, delivery, repair, security
#
# event <id> <kind> [foe:<who>]              (foe: who takes crew on this job, for the ransom letters; "pirates" if
#                                             not said; underscores for spaces: foe:the_rebels)
# <the situation, as many lines as needed>
# * <a choice anyone can take>
# * [<race>] <a choice open with that race along: blue>
# * [fight] <a choice a Mantis, or combat skill, makes safer>   tags: fight (Mantis, combat), tech (Engi, repair),
#                                              heat (Rock, repair), power (Zoltan, engines), cold (Lanius, shields),
#                                              talk (Slug, piloting), fly (piloting), guns (weapons); [rock heat] combine
#   = <effects> | <words>                     the one outcome
#   ~ <weight> <effects> | <words>            one of several, drawn by weight (a bad one, hurting or losing crew, is
#                                             drawn less with a crew member whose race or skill fits the choice)
# step <name>                                 one more choice of the same moment, reached with "then <name>": its
# <its words, or none>                        choices follow the outcome's words on the same screen
# * ...
#
# Effects: scrap 8 or scrap 6-10, fuel 2, missiles 1, parts 2 (drone parts), item weapon|drone|augment, injure 1
# (to the infirmary), lose 1 (dead), taken 1 (a ransom follows), clone 1 (dead, and back from the hiring ship's clone
# bay a level down in every skill), xp <skill> <points> (experience for the crew member the choice is about: pilot,
# engines, shields, weapons, repair, combat; a few points for work done, FTL's levels come at 7 to 65), join
# <race|any>, then <step>.
# In the words: {who} is the crew member the choice is about (the race's, else someone along), {crew} anyone along.
# The game adds "You receive 9 scrap." and the like after the words; don't write them.
#
# Writing them: FTL's beacons, as if the crew were hired and ran into one. One place, one situation; second person,
# short, dry; a few scrap at the end of some branches, nothing at the end of many; a choice that risks crew can
# kill them, and does more often than not. Never FTL's own events or words (the harness holds every line against
# FTL's text). "the rebels" lowercase; the Rebel Flagship is never said to be destroyed.

# ---- postings ----

posting rescue A freighter captain lost their pilot in a rebel raid. The pilot went to ground on a moon in this system, and the captain is going back for them. They need hands, and someone who has seen trouble before.

posting escort A Zoltan merchant's transport needs company for one jump. She has lost two ships this month and says she will not lose a third.
posting escort A small liner wants a second ship beside her through the nebula. The captain says she has had trouble there and won't say what kind.
posting salvage An insurer wants a wrecked freighter in the asteroid belt stripped before the scavengers get to her. Pay on return, by the crate.
posting salvage A dead Mantis raider is drifting around a gas giant. Whoever strips her keeps what they find. Whoever is hiring wants her weapons first.
posting survey A survey company wants readings from an ice moon's southern pole. Simple work, they say. Their last team didn't say anything at all.
posting delivery A sealed crate for a research station on the edge of the system. No questions, no stops. Pay on delivery.
posting delivery Medicine for a farming colony under quarantine. The Federation has cleared the delivery. The colony's governor has not been told.
posting repair An Engi relay station has lost its reactor. The Engi are precise about what they need: four hands, no fear of heat, and no opinions.
posting repair A farming colony's only water pump has stopped. They can pay a little. They can also feed you, which, this far out, is worth more.
posting security A Slug trader is holding an auction on a station at the edge of the nebula and wants a few sturdy people standing near the goods. He says he trusts his customers completely.

event pilot_moon rescue foe:the_rebels
You reach the moon where the pilot is hiding, but a rebel patrol was waiting in orbit. It breaks away and races toward you. The captain who hired you turns from the controls. "Your call. What do we do?"
* [guns] Move in and engage.
  ~ 50 xp weapons 6 then search | The freighter's guns are better than they look, with {who} on them. The patrol breaks off, trailing smoke, and the captain lets out a breath.
  ~ 25 injure 1 | The patrol outguns you. A shot tears through the cargo bay before the captain can pull away, and {who} is under what it brings down. The pilot is left behind.
  ~ 15 lose 1 | The patrol outguns you. A shot tears through the cargo bay before the captain can pull away. {who} was in the cargo bay.
  ~ 10 clone 1 | The patrol outguns you. A shot tears through the cargo bay before the captain can pull away, and {who} was in the cargo bay. The freighter's clone bay has them back on their feet by morning, a little different, and a good deal less skilled. The pilot is left behind.
* Head for the moon and broadcast to the pilot.
  = then search | You dive for the surface, broadcasting on every band. Nothing comes back. Behind you, the patrol is turning for another pass.
* Flee the rebel ship.
  = | The freighter outruns the patrol easily. The captain watches the moon shrink behind you and says nothing the whole way back.
* [mantis fight] {who} offers to beam over to the rebel ship and eliminate the threat.
  ~ 60 scrap 10-14 xp combat 3 | {who} comes back alone and in good spirits. The patrol drifts silent, and the pilot answers your first call.
  ~ 20 injure 1 scrap 10-14 | {who} comes back, though not in one piece. The patrol is silent, and you find the pilot.
  ~ 20 lose 1 scrap 10-14 | The patrol falls silent. {who} does not come back from it. You find the pilot, and the captain pays without a word.

* Keep searching.
  ~ 50 scrap 8-12 xp pilot 4 | A flare goes up from a crater rim. The pilot scrambles aboard, and the captain pays you with shaking hands.
  ~ 20 injure 1 | The patrol catches you on the ground. You get off the moon, but not cleanly: {who} is hit on the way to the hatch, and the pilot is left behind.
  ~ 15 taken 1 | The patrol catches you on the ground. You get off the moon, but {who} is not aboard when you do, and the pilot is still down there.
  ~ 15 lose 1 | The patrol catches you on the ground. {who} is hit on the way to the hatch and doesn't make it through. The pilot is left behind.

  = | The captain doesn't say a word the whole way back.

* Dig with the miners at the entrance.
  = scrap 6-9 | You dig beside the Rock for a day and a night. The miners never speak, but when the trapped crew is brought up alive, one of them puts a hand on your shoulder.
* [heat] Go down the ventilation shaft to reach them from above.
  ~ 45 scrap 10-14 xp repair 3 | The vent is narrow and burning hot, but it's open. You reach the trapped miners hours before the diggers would have.
  ~ 25 injure 1 scrap 6-9 | The vent gives way halfway down. {who} is dragged out by the others, burned to the bone.
  ~ 30 lose 1 scrap 6-9 | The vent gives way halfway down. By the time the others reach {who}, the heat has done its work.
* [rock] {who} walks down the main shaft to find them.
  = scrap 12-16 xp repair 3 | {who} goes into the heat as if it were a warm afternoon, and comes back an hour later with the work crew behind. The families don't have much, but they give you all of it.

  = | They don't argue. They turn back to the miners, who are still digging.
