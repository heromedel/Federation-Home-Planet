# Expeditions: the postings on the board, and the events crew meet on them. This file holds the postings and the
# general events; each sector's own events are in the files it includes.
#
# posting <sector> <danger 1-3> <the posting's words>
# posting sealed <danger 1-3> <words>      a sealed posting: its sector is rolled when posted and told only once under way
#
# event <id> <sector>[,<sector>...] <low|moderate|high> [<theme>]
#   (any = every common sector; a homeworld also meets its race's events. One run meets a theme once.)
# <the event's words, as many lines as needed>
# * <a choice anyone can take>
# * [<race>] <a choice open with that race in the party: blue>
# * [red <race>] <a choice that race brings, and makes worse: red>
# * [fight] <a fight: each Mantis in the party helps, each Rock a little, each Engi hinders>   (tags combine: [mantis fight])
#   ok <effects> | <words>                 a sure outcome, or a gamble:
#   roll <chance of the first, in %>       (each crew member beyond the first adds 5, each injured one takes 3 off)
#   win <effects> | <words>
#   lose <effects> | <words>
# step <name>                               a further pop-up of the same event, reached with "then <name>"
# <its words>
# * ...
#
# Effects: scrap 5-10, injure 1, lose 1, fuel 2, missiles 1, parts 1, item weapon|drone|augment, join <race|any>,
# then <step> (on to that step, not the next event), end (the expedition turns for home).
# In the words: {who} is the crew member the choice is about (the race's, else someone in the party), {crew} anyone
# in the party, {sector} the sector's name.
# Races: human, engi, mantis, rock, slug, zoltan, crystal, lanius. Sectors: civilian, engi, zoltan, mantis, rock, slug,
# nebula, pirate, rebel, abandoned; rare: engi_home, mantis_home, rock_home, zoltan_home, slug_home, crystal.
#
# Writing them: FTL's tone (second person, short, dry, grim more often than funny), never FTL's events or words; a
# familiar thing seen from a new side. Every step has a hook, something at stake and a turn; every choice is a real
# decision. Low risk rarely injures and never kills; high risk can kill, and pays for it. Voice: "the rebels"
# lowercase; the Rebel Flagship is never said to be destroyed. The harness holds every line against FTL's own text.

event stowaway any low
A girl is found curled up behind the water tanks, clutching a stolen ration bar. She begs your crew not to send her back to the labour camp she ran from.
* Take her to the next colony and see her settled
  ok scrap 2-4 | The colony takes her in. Her new guardian presses a few scrap into {crew}'s hand and won't hear a word against it.
* Report her to the camp's overseers
  ok scrap 8-12 | The overseers pay a finder's fee without looking up from their ledgers. Nobody in your crew says much for the rest of the day.
* [human] Let {who} talk to her
  ok scrap 4-6 fuel 2 | She opens up to {who}: the camp keeps its fuel in an unguarded shed. She shows them the way before she goes her own.

event dark_beacon any low navigation
The navigation beacon on the route has gone dark. Without it, the hired shuttle drifts while its pilot works through the manual one page at a time.
* Suit up and repair the beacon by hand
  roll 70
  win scrap 6-10 | The beacon flickers back to life. Its owners pay a technician's rate, and seem surprised anyone bothered.
  lose injure 1 | A loose panel opens {who}'s suit along the forearm. The patch holds, just.
* Wait for a passing ship to tow you
  ok scrap 1-3 | A freighter tows the shuttle on after half a day, and its cook trades a meal for a little help in the galley.
* [engi] Let {who} speak to the beacon directly
  ok scrap 10-14 | {who} plugs into the service port and hums at it for a while. The beacon answers. So does its owner, with a reward.

event bait any moderate ambush
A distress call leads to a gutted transport. Too gutted: the scorch marks are old, and the airlock was opened from outside. Then three armed figures step out from behind the wreck.
* [fight] Fight them
  roll 50
  win scrap 8-15 item weapon | The ambushers break and run, leaving a weapon behind in their hurry.
  lose injure 1 scrap 2-4 | They drive the ambushers off, but {who} takes a hit doing it.
* Hand over the rations and back away
  ok | The ambushers take a crate of rations and let your crew go. Nobody is hurt, and nothing is gained.
* [rock] {who} walks straight at them
  ok scrap 10-14 | Shots spark off {who}'s hide. The ambushers decide this was never worth it.
* [crystal] {who} seals the passage with crystal
  ok scrap 6-10 | A wall of crystal grows across the gap. By the time it fades, the ambushers have given up and gone.

event cargo_pod any moderate salvage
A cargo pod tumbles across the shuttle's path, its markings burned away and its lock still sealed.
* Haul it aboard and crack it open
  roll 60
  win scrap 6-12 missiles 2 | Inside: salvage, and two missiles still in their cradles.
  lose injure 1 | The pod was rigged. The blast throws {who} across the cabin.
* Scan it from a distance and move on
  ok scrap 1-3 | Nothing worth the risk, but the scan data sells at the next stop.
* [zoltan] {who} reads its power signature
  ok scrap 8-12 parts 2 | {who} finds the trap and drains it. The pod holds scrap and spare drone parts.

event fever any moderate plague
Half the passengers on the transport are down with a fever, the ship's medic among them. The captain asks your crew to help, and doesn't ask very hopefully.
* Help nurse them through it
  roll 65
  win scrap 6-10 | The fever breaks in two days. The captain refuses your crew's fare and pays them instead.
  lose injure 1 scrap 4-6 | Everyone pulls through, but {who} catches it and is still weak at journey's end.
* Keep to your own cabin
  ok | Your crew stays healthy and keeps to itself. It is a long, quiet trip.
* [engi] {who} can't catch it
  ok scrap 8-12 | {who} tends the sick day and night, untouched by any fever made for flesh. The captain is very grateful.
* [crystal] {who} offers to stand watch over the worst cases
  ok scrap 8-10 join human | {who} sits with the dying for three nights without sleep. One of the survivors asks to come along when the trip is done.

event old_soldier any low lead
An old Federation veteran shares your crew's table at a stopover. He has stories, a missing hand, and a map he swears leads to a supply cache the fleet left behind.
* Buy him a drink and the map
  roll 50
  win scrap 10-16 fuel 2 then cache | The cache is real: dented, half-looted, but worth the detour.
  lose | The map leads to a bare asteroid. The old man was probably telling the truth, a long time ago.
* Listen to the stories and move on
  ok scrap 1-2 | He tells good stories. He even insists on paying for the round.
step cache
Behind the cache's last crate is a locked locker, stencilled with a unit number nobody uses any more.
* Force it open
  roll 60
  win item weapon | Inside is a weapon, oiled and wrapped as if its owner meant to come back for it.
  lose injure 1 | The lock was wired to a charge. {who} takes the worst of it.
* Leave it for whoever it was meant for
  ok | Your crew closes the cache behind them. Some things are owed to the dead.

event inspection any moderate customs
A customs cutter pulls the transport aside for inspection. The officer finds nothing wrong, then finds it again, slower, and mentions that inspections can take days.
* Offer him something for his trouble
  ok | He accepts a crate of your crew's rations, and the inspection is suddenly complete.
* Wait it out
  roll 55
  win scrap 2-4 | After a day his superior arrives, apologises for the delay, and pays a little for it.
  lose end | The inspection finds a reason to turn the transport back. The expedition is over.
* [slug] {who} has a quiet word with the officer
  ok scrap 6-10 | Whatever {who} picked out of his thoughts, the officer goes pale, waves them on, and pays them to forget his name.
* [zoltan] {who} cites the relevant shipping regulation
  ok scrap 2-4 | {who} recites the inspection code from memory, sub-clause by sub-clause. The officer gives up before the end of the first chapter.

event signal_mast any high derelict
The transport passes a derelict signal mast still broadcasting a Federation code from before the war. Its maintenance pod is pressurised. Something inside moves when the lights come on.
* Board the mast
  roll 45
  win scrap 12-18 then deeper | A family of station rats, and the mast's old stores. But there's a lower deck, and the hatch is warm.
  lose injure 1 | A pressure seal fails as they open it. {who} is caught in the blast of escaping air.
* Log the code and keep going
  ok scrap 2-4 | Federation intelligence pays a little for the location.
step deeper
The lower deck holds a stasis pod, still powered. The face behind the frost is human, and the readout says she has been asleep for nine years.
* Wake her
  roll 60
  win join human | She wakes confused, then furious, then very grateful. She asks to sign on, since everyone she knew is long gone.
  lose | She doesn't wake. Your crew logs her name for the Federation and leaves her as she was.
* Leave the pod sealed and report it
  ok scrap 6-10 | The Federation pays a recovery fee for the report. Someone, somewhere, will be told.
* [engi] {who} checks the pod's systems first
  ok join human | {who} finds the fault in the revival cycle and fixes it before anyone touches the lid. She wakes without a scratch, and signs on.
