# Expeditions: the postings on the board, and the events the commander and crew meet on them.
#
# posting <kind>:<event> <the posting's words>   kinds: rescue, escort, salvage, survey, delivery, repair, security;
#                                             each event has one posting, written for it, and plays when it's taken
#
# event <id> <kind> [foe:<who>]              (foe: who takes crew on this job, for the ransom letters; "pirates" if
#                                             not said; underscores for spaces: foe:the_rebels)
# <the situation, as many lines as needed>
# * <a choice anyone can take>
# * [<race>] <a choice open with that race along: blue>
# * [<what it takes>] <a choice some crew are better at>
#     what it takes: a word for the race that's good at it (fight: Mantis, with combat; tech: Engi, with repair; heat:
#     Rock; power: Zoltan; airless: Lanius; mind: Slug) and a skill by name (pilot, engines, shields, weapons, repair,
#     combat), which overrides the word's: [weapons], [fight], [heat repair], [mantis fight].
#     The crew member best suited takes the choice on: its risk falls on them first, its experience is theirs, and its
#     bad outcomes are rarer for them (half for the race, less again for the skill). A race's own option is always
#     taken by one of that race, so its bad outcomes are always halved. A choice nothing picks for falls to anyone.
#   = <effects> | <words>                     the one outcome
#   ~ <weight> <effects> | <words>            one of several, drawn by weight
# step <name>                                 one more choice of the same moment, reached with "then <name>": its
# <its words, or none>                        choices follow the outcome's words on the same screen
# * ...
#
# Effects: scrap 8 or scrap 6-10, fuel 2, missiles 1, parts 2 (drone parts), item weapon|drone|augment, injure 1
# (to the infirmary), lose 1 (dead), taken 1 (a ransom follows), clone 1 (dead, and back from the hiring ship's clone
# bay a level down in every skill), xp <skill> <points> (experience for whoever took the choice on; a choice anyone can
# take must take that skill, so the right one earns it; FTL's levels come at 7 to 65 points), join <race|any>, then
# <step>.
# In the words: {who} is the crew member who takes the choice on; only a race's option may name them on its button.
# The game adds "You receive 9 scrap." and the like after the words; don't write them.
#
# Writing them: FTL's beacons, as if the crew were hired and ran into one. One place, one situation; second person,
# short, dry; a few scrap at the end of some branches, nothing at the end of many. A choice that risks crew can kill:
# every outcome that hurts someone has a death beside it at least as likely. A clone bay's return is a kind of hurt
# (its weight comes out of the hurt's share, never the death's), and only where the hiring ship could carry one. The
# commander has no ship of their own: where a job needs one, the posting lends it. Never FTL's own events or words
# (the harness holds every line against FTL's text). "the rebels" lowercase; the Rebel Flagship is never said to be
# destroyed. Ships are "she".

# ---- postings ----

posting rescue:pilot_moon A freighter captain lost their pilot in a rebel raid. The pilot went to ground on a moon a few jumps out, and the captain is going back for them. They need hands, and someone who has seen trouble before.
posting rescue:rock_shaft A Rock mining colony has lost a work crew in a shaft collapse. The miners dig slowly and don't ask for help. Their families do.
posting escort:lane_toll A Zoltan merchant needs an escort for one jump, and has an armed cutter for whoever takes the job. She has lost two escorts this month. She says she will not lose a third.
posting escort:dark_liner A small liner wants an escort through the nebula. Her owners will lend a cutter to anyone who'll fly it. The captain says she has had trouble in there, and won't say what kind.
posting salvage:belt_freighter An insurer wants a wrecked freighter in the asteroid belt stripped before the scavengers get to her. Pay on return, by the crate.
posting salvage:mantis_raider A dead Mantis raider is drifting around a gas giant. A salvage broker wants what's in her weapons bay, and says anything else aboard is yours to keep.
posting survey:ice_moon A survey company wants readings from an ice moon's southern pole. Simple work, they say. Their last team didn't say anything at all.
posting delivery:dark_station A sealed crate for a research station two jumps out. No questions, no stops. Pay on delivery.
posting delivery:quarantine Medicine for a farming colony under quarantine. The Federation has cleared the delivery. The colony's governor has not been told.
posting repair:engi_relay An Engi relay station has lost its reactor. The Engi are precise about what they need: four hands, no fear of heat, and no opinions.
posting repair:dry_well A farming colony's only water pump has stopped. They can pay a little. They can also feed you, which, this far out, is worth more.
posting security:slug_auction A Slug trader is holding an auction on a station at the edge of the nebula and wants a few sturdy people standing near the goods. He says he trusts his customers completely.

  ~ 50 xp weapons 6 then search | The freighter's guns are better than they look, with {who} on them. The patrol breaks off, trailing smoke, and the captain lets out a breath.
  ~ 15 injure 1 | The patrol outguns you. Its second shot hits the freighter's gun mount with {who} at it. The captain pulls away, and the pilot is left behind.
  ~ 25 lose 1 | The patrol outguns you. Its second shot takes the freighter's gun mount, and {who} with it. The captain pulls away, and the pilot is left behind.
  ~ 10 clone 1 | The patrol outguns you. Its second shot takes the freighter's gun mount, and {who} with it. The freighter's clone bay has {who} back on their feet by morning, a little different, and a good deal less skilled. The pilot is left behind.
* Head for the moon and broadcast to the pilot.
  = then search | You dive for the surface, broadcasting on every band that the captain has come back for them. Nothing answers. Behind you, the patrol is turning for another pass.
* Flee the rebel ship.
  = | The freighter outruns the patrol easily. The captain watches the moon shrink behind you and says nothing the whole way back.
* [mantis fight] {who} offers to beam over to the rebel ship and eliminate the threat.
  ~ 60 scrap 10-14 xp combat 3 | {who} comes back alone and in good spirits. The patrol drifts silent, and the pilot answers your first call.
  ~ 10 injure 1 scrap 10-14 | {who} comes back, though not in one piece. The patrol is silent, and you find the pilot.
  ~ 20 lose 1 scrap 10-14 | The patrol falls silent. {who} does not come back from it. You find the pilot, and the captain pays without a word.
  ~ 10 clone 1 scrap 10-14 | The patrol falls silent, and so does {who}. The freighter's clone bay brings {who} back that night, a little different, and a good deal less skilled. You find the pilot.

* Keep searching.
  ~ 50 scrap 8-12 | A flare goes up from a crater rim. The pilot scrambles aboard, and the captain pays you with shaking hands.
  ~ 10 injure 1 | The patrol catches you on the ground. You get off the moon, but not cleanly: {who} is hit on the way to the hatch, and the pilot is left behind.
  ~ 10 clone 1 | The patrol catches you on the ground. {who} is hit on the way to the hatch and dies on the deck. The freighter's clone bay gives {who} back, a little different, and a good deal less skilled. The pilot is left behind.
  ~ 15 taken 1 | The patrol catches you on the ground. You get off the moon, but {who} is not aboard when you do, and the pilot is still down there.
  ~ 15 lose 1 | The patrol catches you on the ground. {who} is hit on the way to the hatch and doesn't make it through. The pilot is left behind.
* [slug] {who} closes their eyes and listens for a frightened mind.
  = scrap 8-12 | {who} points at a crater rim. The pilot is there, wedged into a crack in the rock, and comes aboard shaking. The captain pays you before you've left orbit.
* Give up and flee.
  = | The captain takes the freighter up and away, and doesn't look back at the moon.

event rock_shaft rescue
The shaft entrance is a black mouth in a plain of cooled lava, and the air coming out of it is hot enough to bend the light. Two Rock miners are already digging at the mouth, in no hurry. The families stand behind them, watching you.
* Dig with the miners at the entrance.
  = scrap 6-9 | You dig beside the Rock for a day and a night. The miners never speak, but when the trapped crew is brought up alive, one of them puts a hand on your shoulder.
* [heat] Go down the ventilation shaft to reach them from above.
  ~ 45 scrap 10-14 | The vent is narrow and burning hot, but it's open. You reach the trapped miners hours before the diggers would have.
  ~ 25 injure 1 | The vent gives way halfway down. You dig {who} out of it alive, just. The miners get their own people out the slow way.
  ~ 30 lose 1 | The vent gives way halfway down, on top of {who}. By the time you dig through, it's too late. The miners get their own people out the slow way.
* [rock] {who} walks down the main shaft to find them.
  = scrap 12-16 | {who} goes into the heat as if it were a warm afternoon, and comes back an hour later with the work crew behind. The families don't have much, but they give you all of it.
* Tell the families the shaft is beyond you.
  = | They don't argue. They turn back to the miners, who are still digging.

* Pay the toll out of your own pocket.

* [weapons] Refuse, and put the cutter between the scout and the transport.
  ~ 40 scrap 16-20 xp weapons 5 | The scout fires once, to test you. {who} answers with the cutter's guns, and the scout jumps away trailing sparks. The merchant pays you double.
  ~ 20 injure 1 scrap 8-10 | The scout fires first. Its shot hits the cutter's turret with {who} inside, and the scout jumps away before you can answer. The merchant pays.
  ~ 20 lose 1 scrap 8-10 | The scout fires first, and its shot goes clean through the cutter's turret. {who} was in it. The scout jumps away. The merchant pays.
  ~ 20 | The scout isn't alone. A second ship drops in and takes the cargo while you are facing the first. The merchant doesn't pay.
* [slug] {who} listens to the scout's crew for a moment, then tells you they're bluffing.
  = scrap 12-14 | {who} is right. The scout has no working weapons. You tell its captain so, and he leaves without a word.
* Tell the merchant to hand over the cargo.
  = scrap 4-5 | She does, and pays you exactly half of what she promised.
