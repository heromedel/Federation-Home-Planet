event spider_nest civilian,engi high spiders
The colony's survey team found the nest a week ago: a crater on the far moon, roofed over with silk, and something big breathing under it. The colony wants it gone before the hatchlings learn to cross.
* Burn the crater from the rim
  roll 50
  win scrap 12-18 | The silk goes up like paper. Nothing climbs out. The colony pays, and names the crater after nobody.
  lose injure 1 scrap 4-6 then hatch | The fire takes, but not before the hatchlings pour out over the rim.
* Go down and find the queen
  roll 35
  win scrap 18-24 item augment | Under the silk is an old freighter the creatures had been nesting in, and a cargo the colony never knew about.
  lose lose 1 | The queen was waiting. {who} doesn't come back up.
* Tell the colony to move
  ok scrap 2-4 | The colonists listen, grimly. They pay your crew a little for the warning and start packing.
* [rock] {who} carries the fuel down into the nest
  ok scrap 16-22 | {who} walks through the fire and out again, the nest burning behind. The colony watches from the hill in silence.
step hatch
Dozens of hatchlings, each the size of a dog, scatter across the plain toward the colony's fences.
* [fight] Hold the fence line
  roll 55
  win scrap 8-12 | Your crew holds until dawn. The colonists count the bodies and pay a bounty for each.
  lose injure 1 | The fence holds, mostly. {who} was standing where it didn't.
* Fall back to the colony's shelter
  ok | The hatchlings tear through the fields and move on toward the hills. The colony is safe, and the harvest is ruined.

event lost_world civilian moderate spiders
The settlement on the second moon stopped answering two years ago. The Federation's report says "infestation" and nothing more. A grieving father is paying for someone to bring back his daughter's journal.
* Land and search the settlement
  roll 55
  win scrap 8-12 then journal | The streets are wrapped in old silk, long empty. The house is where he said.
  lose injure 1 | Something still lives in the grain silo. {who} doesn't see it until it's too late.
* Search from orbit with the shuttle's scanners
  ok scrap 2-4 | No sign of the journal from up here, and no sign of anything living. The father pays for the effort, and thanks your crew for trying.
* [slug] {who} listens for minds below
  ok scrap 6-8 then journal | {who} hears nothing large down there, only the faint hum of something small and many, and far away.
step journal
The journal is on her desk. The last page is a drawing of the moon's sky, and the date of the first sighting.
* Bring it back unread
  ok scrap 8-12 | The father holds it for a long time before he opens it. He pays double.
* Read it, in case it says what happened
  ok scrap 6-10 | It doesn't say. It's a girl's journal: friends, a dog, a boy she liked. Your crew hands it over and nobody mentions they read it.

* Work the fields
  ok scrap 6-10 | Long days, sore backs, and the crop is in before the storm breaks. The farmers pay in scrap and a feast.
  aside 20 | The farmers name a calf after {crew}. It seems to be an honour.
* Try to repair the machines
  roll 50
  win scrap 12-16 | The harvesters roar back to life. The farmers cheer, then pay double.
  lose scrap 3-5 | The machines are past saving, so your crew picks by hand after all, and earns less for the lost day.
* [engi] {who} rewires the harvesters
  ok scrap 12-18 | {who} has the machines running in an hour, and better than they ran when they were new.

event fugitive civilian moderate bounty
The bounty's target is living as a dockworker under a false name. He looks up from his crate as your crew comes through the gate, and knows.
* Chase him through the docks
  roll 55
  win scrap 14-20 | They catch him at the last airlock. The marshal pays the bounty in full.
  lose injure 1 scrap 2-4 then trail | He gets away on a cargo hauler. {who} took a crate to the shoulder trying to stop him.
* Wait for him at his rented room
  roll 70
  win scrap 10-14 | He comes home that night. He goes quietly.
  lose then trail | He never comes back.
* [human] {who} works beside him for a shift first
  ok scrap 14-18 | {who} hauls crates with him for half a shift, then takes him without a fuss.
  aside 20 | He shakes {who}'s hand as the marshal takes him away. Best shift partner he ever had, he says.
step trail
The hauler's flight plan says it's bound for a mining town two beacons away. The marshal says the bounty stands, but the pay for the trip won't.
* Follow him
  roll 50
  win scrap 12-16 | He thought he'd lost them. He hadn't.
  lose end | The mining town closes ranks around him. Your crew heads home with nothing.
* Let him go
  ok | The marshal shrugs. There are always more names on the board.

  lose scrap 2-4 | It can't be saved. The council pays for the honest answer.

  aside 25 | A colonist's child follows {who} around for the rest of the day, asking to see inside the tanks again.
* [engi] {who} reads the reclaimer's error logs
  ok scrap 10-12 | The logs say it plainly: a cracked filter housing, reported every hour for a month. Nobody had read them.
