THE HOME PLANET STATION
Major changes by version

4B.01 to 4B.99
  Save safety, retired designs, blueprint backups, ship history and restore.
  Prices from FTL's own blueprints, house rules, Refit repairs and upgrades.
  Transmissions inbox, the Federation's letters, reply chains (the One Point of Hull).
  Immersive Mode: careers (Easy, Normal, Hard, Custom), Switch Game Mode, rank, stipend.
  After a final victory: rescue her or give her to the museum.
  Junkyard, derelicts and parts. Check for Updates. Plead for New Ship.
  Long Range Comm. Expeditions and hiring. Reputation. Design Ship in three steps.

5.00 to 5.99
  Days are real: time passes at the station as ships fly or during some other activities. Captain's Quarters and the Captain's Log.
  Crew expeditions, the infirmary and ransoms, the Clone Bay.
  Reputation as a currency. The Third Fleet Commander.
  Playing FTL docked in the station's window. The console.
  The Crew Log. Updates to the Refit tab and the Cargo Bay.
  The fleet on disk and the logs rebuilt toward 6.0. The station's words move into lore/.

6.00 to 6.10
  Storage, logs and code rebuilt; older fleets convert on first opening.
  Faster Board and Dock. Station Log in the station's own words.
  Ship marks: each ship records her career and origin.

6.11 to 6.20
  Updates to the vault, crew files and the logs.
  Reputation is earned at each career's rate, by ship difficulty and sector.

6.21 to 6.32
  Distress signals earn reputation. Stardates replace calendar dates.
  Updates to pop-ups and tooltips.

6.33 to 6.41
  Optional crew name lists by race. Engi Translate added.
  Expedition roles, fixed training and results told by job.
  Updates to the Captain's Log.

6.42 to 6.52
  Updates to Refit and Remodel, with a clickable Power Distribution Simulation.
  Ship Pronoun setting. Final victory choices: Nothing, Choice or Museum.
  Updates to Custom careers. Build A Release.bat added.

6.53 to 6.60
  Updates to Design Ship, the house rules and the settings.
  Updates to docked FTL and tooltips. Admiral comes at 5,000 reputation.

6.61 to 6.64
  Optional infirmary setting, with a chance to save a crewmate from death.
  Deadlock fix. First User Manual.

6.65 to 6.72
  Updates to the One Point of Hull letters, expedition rewards and reports.
  Posting for crew ends the day. Junkyard parts search.
  Updates to the Cargo Bay, ship values and the Captain's Log.
  Manual built in text and HTML.

6.73 to 6.80
  Manual opens from Settings, with Find, dark mode and Top links.
  Search added to the station log, ship logs and Career Reputation Log.

6.81 to 7.00
  The manual in the station's own window. Every move of more than one file is one protection note.
  7.00: support for fleets and careers from before 6.0 removed; Long Range Comm. protocol 3.
  7.01: the code's shared helpers (one XML reader, one place for what goes aboard the Cargo Hold); nothing a player sees changes.
  7.02: the vault's code split along its seams, long methods split, stale comments removed; nothing a player sees changes.
  7.03: the watch on FTL's continue.sav keeps its own notes (Steam Cloud's copies, New Game's overwrite); nothing a player sees changes.
  7.04: the vault back as it was at 7.01, one class in charge of the fleet (7.02's split had its helpers reaching into the fleet's list); everything else from 7.02 kept. Nothing a player sees changes.

7.05 to 7.08
  7.06: small wording fixes (a/an in letters, a design name starting with "The", the race named in the hiring log lines).
  7.07: the last pieces kept only for fleets from before 6.0 removed. Such a fleet is not read: a 6.x station has to open it once first, so the 6.00 note above no longer applies.

7.09 to 7.11
  7.11: Other... has Close Station Interface, which asks the same question as Esc at the Space Dock.
